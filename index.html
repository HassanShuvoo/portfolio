About Me

Short Bio

Dr. Bhuiyan is a Professor of Computer Science (Cybersecurity and Trustworthy AI/ML) at Fordham University, New York, and Director of the DependSys Research Lab, affiliated with the Fordham Center for Cybersecurity. His research focuses on cybersecurity & privacy, trustworthy AI/ML, data-driven dependability, and secure CPS/IoT systems. He has authored over 250 publications with more than 14,000 citations, including journal articles, conference papers, books, and book chapters, in leading venues such as IEEE and ACM transactions. He has served as an Associate Editor for multiple top journals and in leadership roles for major conferences. He has been named a “Highly Cited Researcher” and a “Rising Star of Science.”

Position & Leadership

Dr. Bhuiyan is a tenured Associate Professor in the Department of Computer and Information Sciences at Fordham University, New York, USA. He also serves as the Director of the Dependable and Secure Systems Research (DependSys) Lab and is affiliated with the Fordham Center for Cybersecurity. Prior to joining Fordham, he held faculty positions as an Assistant Professor at Fordham University and Temple University, and as an Adjunct Professor at Harvard University.

Research & Scholarly Impact

His research spans cybersecurity and privacy, trustworthy AI/ML, data-driven system dependability, and emerging CPS/IoT systems. Dr. Bhuiyan has authored and co-authored over 250 publications, including journal and transactions articles, conference papers, 10 book chapters, and 4 books. His work has appeared in leading venues such as IEEE TC, TPDS, TII, TDSC, TITS, TSUSC, TETC, IoT-J, ACM ToIT, TCPS, and journals such as JSA, INS, JPDC, and JNCA. He has also published in premier conferences including IEEE INFOCOM, IEEE SECON, IEEE/IFIP DSN, IEEE SRDS, IEEE TrustCom, DCOSS, IEEE MASS, and IEEE DASC.

Editorial Roles & Recognition

Dr. Bhuiyan has served as an Associate Editor and Lead Guest Editor for several prominent journals, including IEEE Transactions on Industrial Informatics, ACM Transactions on Multimedia Computing, Communications, and Applications (TOMM), ACM Transactions on Cyber-Physical Systems, IEEE Internet of Things Journal, Future Generation Computer Systems, Journal of Network and Computer Applications, Journal of Parallel and Distributed Computing, and Computer Networks. He has been recognized as a Highly Cited Researcher by Web of Science, named among the Rising Stars of Science, and received the IEEE TCSC Early Career Researcher Award, IEEE Outstanding Leadership Award, and multiple IEEE Service Awards.

Service to the Community

He has served in major leadership and organizational roles including General Chair, Program Chair, Workshop Chair, Publicity Chair, Technical Program Committee member, and reviewer for numerous international conferences and journals. His service includes roles in IEEE DASC 2024, INFOCOM AidTSP Workshop, IEEE TrustCom (2023, 2020), IEEE CSCloud 2022, IEEE iSCI 2021, and IEEE DependSys 2020.
