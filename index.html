About Me

Short Bio

Dr. Bhuiyan is a Professor of Computer Science (Cybersecurity and Trustworthy AI/ML) at Fordham University, New York, and Director of the DependSys Research Lab, affiliated with the Fordham Center for Cybersecurity. His research focuses on cybersecurity & privacy, trustworthy AI/ML, data-driven dependability, and secure CPS/IoT systems. He has authored over 250 publications with more than 14,500 citations, including journal articles, conference papers, books, and book chapters, in leading venues such as IEEE and ACM transactions. He has served as an Associate Editor for multiple top journals and in leadership roles for major conferences. He has been named a “Highly Cited Researcher” and a “Rising Star of Science.”

Position & Leadership

Dr. Bhuiyan is a Professor in the Department of Computer and Information Sciences at Fordham University, New York, USA. He also serves as the Director of the Dependable and Secure Systems Research (DependSys) Lab and is affiliated with the Fordham Center for Cybersecurity. Prior to joining Fordham, he held faculty positions as an Assistant Professor at Fordham University and Temple University, and as an Adjunct Professor at Harvard University.

Research & Scholarly Impact

His research spans cybersecurity and privacy, trustworthy AI/ML, data-driven system dependability, and emerging CPS/IoT systems. Dr. Bhuiyan has authored and co-authored over 250 publications, including journal and transactions articles, conference papers, 10 book chapters, and 4 books. His work has appeared in leading IEEE/ACM journals and conferences.

Editorial Roles & Recognition

Dr. Bhuiyan has served as an Associate Editor and Lead Guest Editor for several prominent journals, and has received research, education and service recognitions.
