Research Areas

Core themes powering dependable, secure, and human-centered intelligent systems.

  • Cybersecurity & Privacy | Data-Driven Dependability
  • Trustworthy and Responsible AI/ML
  • Wireless Sensing, Biometrics, and Authentication
  • Behavioral Data Mining and Intelligent Modeling
  • ML for Security in IoT/CPS and HCI
  • Post-Quantum Security (PQS) and Secure Quantum ML (SQML)
  • Secure and Trustworthy Sensing for UAV/Drone/Self-driven Vehicle
  • Emerging IoT/CPS applications: healthcare monitoring, event monitoring, structural health monitoring, and sensing application

The lab focuses on building robust security and intelligence systems that can be trusted in real-world environments. Work combines theory, system design, and deployment-centered validation.

Active Research Projects

Leading-edge exploration in security, dependability, and intelligent computing paradigms.

Trustworthy AI & Resilient IoT/CPS Frameworks

Developing robust security protocols for Cyber-Physical Systems (CPS) while ensuring the transparency and privacy of AI/ML models integrated into the Internet of Things.

Secure Signal Intelligence & Physical-Layer Authentication

Advancing methods for secure wireless signal collection and engineering, with a focus on hardware-level authentication and signal integrity.

Mitigating Algorithmic Bias in Autonomous AI Systems

Investigating the socio-technical drivers of bias in AI algorithms to develop fairness-aware machine learning models.

Transitioning to Quantum-Resilient Architectures (PQC & SQML)

Leading research into Post-Quantum Cryptography (PQC) and the development of Secure Quantum Machine Learning (SQML) to defend against emerging cryptographic threats.

Decentralized Security via Blockchain Integration

Exploring the application of blockchain for distributed ledger security and data provenance in large-scale networks.
